# Glossary

**Multisig**: requires multiple signers.

**M-of-N**: M approvals needed out of N signers.

**Threshold**: minimum approvals to execute a proposal.

**Proposal**: pending action awaiting approvals.

**Signer**: authorized vault member.

**Vault**: the multisig treasury contract.

**Approval**: signer confirming a proposal.

**Expiry**: deadline after which a proposal cannot execute.
